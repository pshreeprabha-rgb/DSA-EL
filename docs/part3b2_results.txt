=== 7. OBSERVATIONS (SINGLE SEED = 42, NO SCENARIOS) ===
- With --spacing time and no scenarios (seed 42), greedy_spec_ma (817.70 s,
  10 forced dumps, 4 moves), greedy_marginal_ma (822.53 s, 45 forced dumps,
  7 moves), and greedy_marginal_oracle (822.53 s, 45 forced dumps, 7 moves)
  have a higher mean wait than fixed_equal (750.54 s, 0 forced dumps, 0 moves),
  while greedy_marginal_rf has 731.68 s (32 forced dumps, 6 moves) and
  fixed_proportional has 754.38 s (0 forced dumps, 0 moves).
- In Section 6 above (--spacing stop, no scenarios, seed 42), greedy_spec_ma
  (1207.18 s, 3 forced dumps, 3 moves), greedy_marginal_ma (1200.11 s, 13 forced
  dumps, 7 moves), and greedy_marginal_oracle (1200.11 s, 13 forced dumps,
  7 moves) also have a higher mean wait than fixed_equal (1136.50 s, 0 forced
  dumps, 0 moves), while greedy_marginal_rf has 1119.12 s (7 forced dumps,
  6 moves) and fixed_proportional has 1186.34 s (0 forced dumps, 0 moves).
  These runs use a single seed (seed = 42) and the cause is unmeasured; any
  earlier --spacing stop run showing the opposite is not in this checkout.
- Forced dumps remove onboard passengers from a reassigned bus (b->load = 0,
  forced_dumps += b->load) without any wait-time penalty or re-queuing.
