=== 3. OBSERVATIONS (SINGLE SEED = 42, NO SCENARIOS) ===
- Both runs above use a single PRNG seed (seed = 42) with default network
  settings (--stops 60 --routes 6 --buses 36 --spacing time).
- Under --reassign dump, all 6 policy rows reproduce the raw numbers in
  docs/part3b2_results.txt Section 3 (greedy_spec_ma: 10 forced dumps, 817.70 s
  mean wait; greedy_marginal_ma and greedy_marginal_oracle: 45 forced dumps,
  822.53 s mean wait; greedy_marginal_rf: 32 forced dumps, 731.68 s mean wait).
- Under --reassign drain, forced_dumps is 0 across all 6 policies while the
  same move counts apply (4, 7, 6, 7), and observed mean wait times on seed 42
  are 785.46 s (greedy_spec_ma), 781.87 s (greedy_marginal_ma and
  greedy_marginal_oracle), and 731.02 s (greedy_marginal_rf), compared to
  750.54 s for fixed_equal. Cause of wait-time differences is unmeasured.
- In DUMP mode, forced dumps remove onboard passengers without any wait-time
  penalty or re-queuing; in DRAIN mode, a reassigned bus stops boarding and
  continues alighting passengers until load reaches 0 before deadheading.
